Privacy Policy

Post Scheduler is an internal tool built for EasyPC / Tech It Easy Ventures Inc. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

What we access

When you connect a Facebook, YouTube, or TikTok account, the app requests only the permissions needed to publish content on your behalf — for example, basic account/profile identification and the ability to post to the connected Page, channel, or account.

What we store

The app stores: the connected account's name and platform ID, an encrypted access token used to publish posts, scheduled post content (captions, media, timing), and uploaded media files. No personal data beyond what's needed to identify and post to the connected business account is collected.

How data is used

Stored data is used exclusively to publish scheduled content to the accounts you connected. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose outside the operation of this scheduling tool.

Data security

Access tokens are encrypted before storage. Only authorized EasyPC staff have access to the app and its data.

Data retention

You may disconnect an account or delete scheduled posts and uploaded media at any time, which removes the associated data.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the EasyPC digital operations team.
